Toolbox Runs on your device

Unlock PDF & add a visual signature

Open a password-protected PDF with its password, optionally place a typed or drawn signature mark on a page, and save a copy. Your document stays in your browser.

This browser-only tool does not create certificate-backed signatures or change Adobe's verification status. Saving creates a flattened copy, which removes any existing digital signatures, including a UIDAI signature. To verify an original Aadhaar signature, keep the original PDF unchanged and verify its signer certificate in Adobe. Enter the PDF password only if you have it; passwords are never guessed or bypassed.
